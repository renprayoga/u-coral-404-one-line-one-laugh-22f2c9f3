Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Line, One Laugh** — one funny line, a
joke, a quip or a punchy observation, capped at 200 characters. The line
is the whole entry.

## The entry

> I told my plants I'm going on holiday and one of them wilted on the spot — even my ficus has separation anxiety.

112 characters, one line, written from scratch for this bounty.

## Strategy (why this line)

The judge weighs, in order: hilarious line on first read, surprise turn,
economy, freshness, clean wording.

- **Hilarious on first read:** a plant wilting the instant it hears about
  a holiday — the joke lands without setup or explanation.
- **Surprise:** the turn is the diagnosis — "separation anxiety" — a
  therapist's phrase applied to a houseplant. The reader expects a
  watering joke and gets a clingy ficus instead.
- **Economy:** 112 of 200 characters, setup and punchline in one sentence,
  no word wasted; "on the spot" does the timing, "even my ficus" does
  the escalation.
- **Fresh:** not a stock plant joke (no "I kill every plant I own") — the
  plant is needy, not dead, and the specific detail (ficus, not "plant")
  makes it a line nobody has read before.
- **Clean wording:** plain grammar, one rhythm, reads smoothly aloud; the
  em dash marks the turn without slowing the read.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and self-validates it against the
gate: exactly one line, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace.
Deterministic — the line is a constant; no input, no network, no
randomness. Python 3 standard library only.
